Load and shortage in kWh. Actual load, total cost (Energy Expense) and blended rate (Blended Cost with Generation) come from the UAMPS Power Bills workbooks. * = no bill yet, load from meters (complete months only). Actual shortage is blank until a bill figure is chosen. Predicted Total Cost (Net) = energy cost − PX sell-back − spinning reserve revenue. Assumption: PX sell-back price and generator spinning reserve capacity payment are each half of the month's shortage price, unless you enter a price. Sell-back is paid on supply above load, up to the contract volume that hour. Spinning reserve is paid on the part of the 21 MW Lehi gens not running that hour.
Predicted Peak & Shortage by Month
Peak = highest predicted hourly load in the month. Shortage = remaining shortage after internal generation. Min / max avg shortage = the hour of the day with the lowest / highest shortage, averaged over every day of the month. % shortage = monthly shortage ÷ monthly load.

Optimal Purchase Plan — Minimum Net Cost
Optimal = the added HLH MW and LLH MW (whole MW, 0–150) that give the lowest net cost for the month, on top of the contracts already in the plan. HLH = HE 8–23 Mon–Sat excluding holidays; LLH = all other hours. Assumptions: added HLH and LLH are bought at the higher of the Palo or Mid-C futures price for that month + $10/MWh (the adder is editable, or enter a fixed HLH/LLH price to override). Each month's futures price is used for that month in every year (futures from data/market_prices.csv: Dec 2026–Nov 2027); shortage is costed at the month's shortage price; Lehi gens cover up to 21 MW of shortage at the internal gen rate; PX sell-back and spinning reserve are each half the month's shortage price unless you enter a price. Net cost = energy cost (including the purchase) − sell-back revenue − spinning reserve revenue. Savings = net cost with no added purchase − optimal net cost.
